# Can ChatGPT Design Your Dream Desk?

### A practical prompt guide by CVisiona

> Design it. Find the gear. Check the fit. Visualise the result.

You can use ChatGPT to explore a desk setup before buying the equipment. This guide takes you from an inspiration image to a shopping shortlist, a dimensioned layout, and a finished visual concept.

**Use the four prompts in order, in the same conversation.** Upload your reference image first, and replace the bracketed fields with your own information.

## 01 · Design the setup

**Video moment:** “Tell ChatGPT exactly what you want.”

### Copy this prompt

```text
Help me design a practical desk setup using the attached image as inspiration.

My details:
- Desk width × depth × thickness: [cm]
- Space behind the desk: [cm]
- Budget for new purchases: [amount and currency]
- Country for shopping: [country]
- Equipment I already own: [exact models]
- Main activities: [coding, writing, editing, etc.]
- Preferred style: [colours, materials, lighting]

First ask for any essential missing measurements. Then propose a layout,
including monitor positions, seated position, lighting, cable routing,
and a list of equipment to keep or buy.

Distinguish what you can see in the image from what needs verification.
Do not infer desk dimensions or product compatibility from the image alone.
```

```text
Use the right screen as the primary display, directly in front of my
```

**Expected output:** a practical design brief and a list of unanswered questions—not a premature guarantee that everything fits.

## 02 · Find the right gear

**Video moment:** “Find models on Amazon that match your specs and budget.”

### Copy this prompt

```text
Search Amazon [country] for products matching our agreed desk plan.
My remaining budget is [amount and currency]. Keep my existing equipment.

For each recommended purchase, provide:
- Exact brand and model
- Direct product link
- Current price and date checked
- Relevant dimensions and compatibility requirements
- Why it suits this setup
- Anything still needing confirmation

Use manufacturer documentation to verify technical specifications.
For monitor arms, check per-screen weight capacity, VESA support,
screen-size restrictions, reach, height adjustment, and clamp clearance.

Give me one recommended shopping list and one lower-cost alternative,
with totals. Separate optional accessories from essential purchases.
Do not invent links, prices, availability, or specifications.
If browsing is unavailable, ask me for product links instead.
```

## 03 · Check the fit before buying

**Video moment:** “The right gear still has to fit on your desk.”

### Copy this prompt

```text
Check whether the selected equipment physically fits our desk plan.

Use verified external monitor dimensions, not just diagonal sizes or
visible screen dimensions. Account for curvature and inward angles.
Check desk depth, combined footprint, acceptable overhang, arm reach,
clamp clearance, rear clearance, and keyboard and mouse space.

Create a dimensioned plan in SVG or another precise drawing format:
1. Front view
2. Top view
3. Side view

Use the same desk, equipment, single dual monitor arm, mounting point,
and positions in every view. Label dimensions in centimetres.
Distinguish verified measurements from assumptions.

Centre the primary monitor on my seated position. Check whether the
selected arm supports vertical centre alignment of both screens.

End with a checklist labelled confirmed, conditional, or incompatible.
For missing information, tell me exactly what to measure or verify.
Do not use a photorealistic image as evidence of physical fit.
```

```text
The reference lists visible screen dimensions of approximately:

These are reference-image labels, not verified external dimensions.

measurements are available. The visible widths total about 149.4 cm

Show vertical centre alignment. Explain the approximate 2.9 cm extension
of the taller visible screen above and below the shorter one.

Keep one consistent dual-arm base across the front, top, and side views.
The reference image contains inconsistent arm arrangements; correct that

Desk measurements: [cm]
Verified monitor specifications: [links or measurements]

```

## Before you click “Buy”

- [ ] Measure the desktop and available rear space.
- [ ] Confirm exact monitor model names and external dimensions.
- [ ] Check monitor weight without stands and VESA mounting requirements.
- [ ] Check the arm’s capacity, reach, and supported configurations.
- [ ] Inspect the desk edge and underside for clamp obstructions.
- [ ] Allow space for cables, connectors, keyboard, and mouse.
- [ ] Check current prices, delivery, and return conditions.

**A visual concept helps you choose a look. Verified measurements help you choose equipment that fits.**
