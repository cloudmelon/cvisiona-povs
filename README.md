# CVisiona POVs

CVisiona is a next-generation tech media platform founded by [Mélony Qin](https://github.com/cloudmelon). It explains AI, cloud computing, cloud-native technology and the startups building on them through short, visual-first stories: tech in minutes, not hours.

Our mission is to help young professionals and founders shape the future through technology.

This repository collects CVisiona's **POVs**: our points of view on where AI, cloud-native and the startup world are heading, with the notes and sources behind them.

## Where to find CVisiona

### 🌐 Website: [cvisiona.com](https://cvisiona.com)

Articles, explainers and opinion pieces, organised in three sections:

- **Artificial Intelligence**: AI agents, AI infrastructure, AI robotics and AI startups.
- **Cloud Native**: Kubernetes, containers, serverless and open source.
- **Cloud Computing**: Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services.

The site also has videos and a podcast.

### 💌 Newsletter: [Innovators Weekly](https://newsletter.cloudmelonvision.com)

A weekly newsletter for cloud-native and AI innovators: the latest cloud-native, serverless and generative AI stories, told through visual storytelling and hands-on tutorials, to help you build and scale an AI business with cloud-native technology. Unsubscribe at any time.

## About the founder

Mélony Qin is a former Microsoft product manager turned entrepreneur, a CNCF Ambassador, a Kubernetes release team member and the author of four technical books on Kubernetes and Microsoft Azure. Alongside CVisiona, she is building [Cerebro](https://gocerebro.ai), the trust layer between people and AI.
